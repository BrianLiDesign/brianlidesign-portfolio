## Constraints

- Preserve the light notebook aesthetic.
- Keep Hawaii references subtle and tied to useful systems for real people.
- Do not add tropical imagery or generic decorative motifs.
- Keep KERES abstract and simulation-first. Do not imply physical deployment or operational performance.
- Preserve the systems-notebook structure from the selected Mission Replay Lab direction: white field, mono instrumentation, teal state, and thin evidence dividers.
- Keep the homepage focused on three proof projects until the design is intentionally expanded.
