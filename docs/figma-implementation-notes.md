## Constraints

- Preserve the light notebook aesthetic.
- Keep Hawaii references subtle and tied to useful systems for real people.
- Do not add tropical imagery or generic decorative motifs.
- Keep the ReBalance demo calmer than the raw signal visualization.
- Keep the homepage focused on three proof projects until the design is intentionally expanded.
