# Brian Li Portfolio

This portfolio presents Brian Li's work, experience, and professional direction to hiring evaluators while retaining the personal context behind the work.

## Language

**Canonical career facts**:
The titles, dates, metrics, and outcomes stated in the current resume. LinkedIn provides personality and supporting context but does not override conflicting resume facts.
_Avoid_: Profile facts, LinkedIn facts

**Professional through-line**:
Software test and systems engineer building reliable hardware/software systems.
_Avoid_: General computer engineering student, generic software engineer

**Organization mark**:
The full-color, normal brand mark used to identify an employer or organization in an experience entry.
_Avoid_: Company icon, monochrome logo

**Role icon fallback**:
A consistent semantic symbol used only when a suitable organization mark is unavailable.
_Avoid_: Placeholder logo, decorative company mark

**Narrative-led About page**:
An evaluator-facing personal story supported by career evidence, rather than a reproduction of the resume.
_Avoid_: Expanded resume, employment timeline

**Experience scope**:
A concise account of what Brian owned, built, tested, or supported in a role. Quantified outcomes strengthen the scope when reliable but are not mandatory.
_Avoid_: Mandatory impact metric, keyword summary

**Formal education**:
The Education section containing both Cal Poly and Punahou, even when an institution is omitted from the one-page resume.
_Avoid_: Resume-only education

**Leadership & Community**:
The About-page section for Brian's current non-employment leadership roles, including EMPOWER Project Lead and Hack4Impact Cal Poly Tech Lead.
_Avoid_: Work experience, extracurriculars

**Provisional role scope**:
Role-informed descriptive copy supported by available project evidence but not yet confirmed as Brian's exact responsibilities or outcomes.
_Avoid_: Verified accomplishment, inferred metric
